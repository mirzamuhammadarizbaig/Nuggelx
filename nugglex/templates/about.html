How NUGGLEX works

NUGGLEX is a RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) chatbot. It only answers from the restaurant's menu PDF, so it doesn't make stuff up.

  1. Read the PDFThe menu PDF is loaded once when the server starts.
  2. Chunk and indexThe text is split into small pieces and turned into TF-IDF vectors.
  3. RetrieveYour question is matched against the chunks and the closest ones are picked.
  4. AnswerThose chunks plus your question go to an LLM through the Ollama API.

Stack: Flask, Ollama API, scikit-learn, vanilla JS.
